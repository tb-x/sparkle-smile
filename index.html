Turn the phone sideways for bigger teeth.

For grown-ups: drag a finger (or move the mouse) back and forth over a tooth to brush it. Clean teeth get a bubble shield for a while, and germs can't touch them. A clean, shielded tooth slowly heals and its holes fill back in. Sweets (candy, gummies, chocolate) are the worst: they stick hardest, call germs fastest and cause the most damage. Cookies are in the middle; apples, carrots, broccoli and cheese bring only a germ or two. A tooth that gets too many holes falls out. Tap the tube when the toothpaste runs low. One round lasts 2 minutes, like real brushing. On iPhone, flip the silent switch off to hear the sounds.
